Question 1

Section 6.1

True/False: since the area within 2 standard deviations of the mean is \(0.95\), the area more than 2 SD above the mean is \(0.05\).
Show answer

Answer. False.

Why. About \(95\%\) lies between \(\mu-2\sigma\) and \(\mu+2\sigma\). The remaining \(5\%\) splits into two tails, so the upper tail alone is about \(0.025\), not \(0.05\).

Question 2

Section 6.1 / 6.2

Trial length \(X\) is normal with mean \(22\) days and SD \(5\) days. Round probabilities to 4 decimals; the percentile to a whole number.
  1. What is the distribution of \(X\)?
  2. \(P(X\ge 21)\)
  3. \(P(23\le X\le 27)\)
  4. \(67\%\) of trials finish within how many days?

Desmos: normaldist(22,5) with Left / Right / Inner, or .inversecdf.

Show answer

Useful formulas:

\[ z=\frac{x-\mu}{\sigma},\quad P(a<X<b)=\Phi\!\left(\frac{b-\mu}{\sigma}\right)-\Phi\!\left(\frac{a-\mu}{\sigma}\right),\quad x=\mu+z\sigma. \]
  1. \(X\sim N(22,\ 5)\)
  2. \(z=-0.2\), so \(P(X\ge 21)=\Phi(0.2)=\mathbf{0.5793}\)
  3. \(z_{23}=0.2\), \(z_{27}=1\), so \(P=\Phi(1)-\Phi(0.2)=\mathbf{0.2621}\)
  4. \(z_{0.67}\approx 0.4399\), \(x\approx 24.20\to\mathbf{24}\) days

Question 3

Section 7.1

True/False: if the population has mean \(12\) and nonzero SD, then \(P(X>14)\) for one value is always less than \(P(\bar X>14)\) for \(n=44\).
Show answer

Answer. False.

Sample means use \(\mathrm{SE}=\sigma/\sqrt{n}\), which is smaller than \(\sigma\), so \(\bar X\) is tighter around \(12\) and \(P(\bar X>14)<P(X>14)\).

Question 15

Section 8.1

True/False: if \(n\) increases and SD and confidence level stay the same, the margin of error increases.
Show answer

Answer. False.

\[\mathrm{EBM}=z\,\sigma/\sqrt{n}\] shrinks as \(n\) grows.
