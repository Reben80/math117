Book: the exam scores range from 65 to 75. Since 73 is between them, substitute. ŷ = −173.51 + 4.83(73) = 179.08. We predict that statistics students who earn 73 on the third exam will earn 179.08 on the final, on average.

Two words

Inside the data, or past it

INTERPOLATION

An x between the ones you saw.

66 and 73 are between 65 and 75. The predictions 145.27 and 179.08 are interpolation.

EXTRAPOLATION

An x outside the ones you saw.

90 is extrapolation. So is a third-exam score of 50. Do not trust the y the line prints.

Dive times: 50 → 8060 → 5570 → 4580 → 3590 → 25100 → 22. The depths stop at 100, so the 4.7 minutes was extrapolation. The arithmetic was right. The prediction is not one to trust.

Book: predicting inside the observed x values is interpolation. Predicting outside them is extrapolation. Try It 12.6 asked for 110 feet. The observed depths stop at 100, so 4.7 minutes was an extrapolation. Say so here, not as a correction of the arithmetic.
