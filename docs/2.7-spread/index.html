Standard deviation is never negative. It is small when the values hug the mean. In practice, use Desmos. Concentrate on what the number tells you. stdev(L) for a sample, stdevp(L) for a population.

Rosa and Binh, both at store A

How many standard deviations away?

ROSA WAITS 7 MINUTES

7 − 5 = 2 minutes above the mean.

2 ÷ 2 = 1 standard deviation above.

Book’s way: 5 + (1)(2) = 7

BINH WAITS 1 MINUTE

1 − 5 = −4, so 4 minutes below the mean.

−4 ÷ 2 = −2 standard deviations.

Book’s way: 5 + (−2)(2) = 1

Store A: mean 5, standard deviation 2. Think of 2 minutes as one step. Rosa is one step right of 5. Binh is two steps left. Book’s formula: value = mean + (number of steps) × (size of one step). Two or more steps from the mean is a rough flag that a value is far.

The number of standard deviations need not be a whole number. For a sample the symbols are x-bar and s. For a population they are μ and σ. The empirical rule, about 95% within two standard deviations for a bell shape, comes later. Do not teach it as a fact for every shape.

Sample or population

Divide by n − 1, or by N

SAMPLE

The variance divides by n − 1. The standard deviation is s. In Desmos, stdev(L).

Say it: s is “ess.” The mean x̄ is “x-bar.”

POPULATION

The variance divides by N. The standard deviation is σ. In Desmos, stdevp(L).

Say it: σ is the Greek letter “sigma.” The mean μ is “mew.”

The deviations from the mean add to zero, so the calculation squares them. Dividing a sample by n − 1 estimates the population variance better than dividing by n. Let Desmos do that arithmetic.

Variance is in squared units. The standard deviation is the square root, back in the units of the data. s estimates σ when the sample looks like the population. In Desmos: type L = [values], then mean(L) and stdev(L). stdevp(L) is the population version. Next is Example 2.32. The fifth-graders are a sample, so use stdev.

The 20 fifth-graders are a sample, not every fifth-grader.

THE SAMPLE YOU JUST USED

1   What does stdevp divide by?

✓ N, the whole population.

2   Which one should you use?

✓ stdev. A sample divides by n − 1.

✓ When the list is everyone you are describing.
