A symmetric list

Mean, median, and mode are all 7

4, 5, 6, 6, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 7, 8, 8, 8, 9, 10. A vertical line at 7 splits this picture into mirror images. In a perfectly symmetric shape, the mean and the median agree.

Sixteen values. Sum 112, mean 7. The 8th and 9th ordered values are both 7, so the median is 7. Seven appears six times, so the mode is 7. A symmetric bimodal picture is different: the two modes would not equal the mean and the median.

Skewed left

The mean is the smallest of the three

4, 5, 6, 6, 6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 8. Mean 6.3. Median 6.5. Mode 7. The right side looks chopped off. The mean is pulled toward the tail.

Sum of the ten values is 63, so the mean is 6.3. Ordered, the 5th is 6 and the 6th is 7, so the median is 6.5. Seven appears four times.

Skewed right

The mean is the largest of the three

6, 7, 7, 7, 7, 8, 8, 8, 9, 10. Mean 7.7. Median 7.5. Mode 7. The mean reflects the skew more than the median does.

Sum is 77, mean 7.7. The 5th value is 7 and the 6th is 8, so the median is 7.5. The mode is 7.

The pattern

Often, not always

SKEWED LEFT

The mean is often below the median. The tail points left.

SYMMETRIC

The mean and the median are about equal.

SKEWED RIGHT

The mean is often above the median. The tail points right.

This is not true for every list. The usual exceptions are discrete data. The median stays nearer the high point. The mean rides out on the tail.

Example 2.31, letter counts. Terry: 7, 9, 3, 3, 3, 4, 1, 3, 2, 2. Mean 3.7, median 3, right skew. Davis: 3, 3, 3, 4, 1, 4, 3, 2, 3, 1. Mean 2.7, median 3, left skew. Maris: 2, 3, 4, 4, 4, 6, 6, 6, 8, 3. Mean 4.6, median 4, roughly symmetric. The book's summary: the median stays near the high point, and the mean sits farther out on the tail.
