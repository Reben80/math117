Where a value sits

SECTION 2.3

Prof. Rebin Muhammad

Montgomery College

Order the data first. Q2 is the median. Q1 is the median of the lower half. Q3 is the median of the upper half. This is the book's half method, not a different percentile formula.

A percentile is not a percent score

90th percentile is not a grade of 90

WHAT IT MEANS

90% of the scores are the same as yours or lower. 10% are the same or higher.

THE BOOK’S SAT EXAMPLE

A college that wants the 75th percentile of the SAT is asking for a score of at least 1220.

The book’s college example is the 75th percentile, at least 1220, not the 90th. Percentiles are used with large populations. Quartiles cut an ordered list into four pieces: about one quarter on or below Q1, about half on or below Q2, about three quarters on or below Q3.

Example 2.16 · 29 ordered ages

Find the place, then read the age

i = (k / 100) × (n + 1). Order the list first.

70TH PERCENTILE

i = (70 / 100) × (29 + 1) = 21.

21 is a whole number. The 21st age is 64.

83RD PERCENTILE

i = (83 / 100) × 30 = 24.9.

Average the 24th and 25th ages, 71 and 72. The percentile is 71.5.

If i is not a whole number, round it down and up, then average those two ages.

Ordered ages: 18, 21, 22, 25, 26, 27, 29, 30, 31, 33, 36, 37, 41, 42, 47, 52, 55, 57, 58, 62, 64, 67, 69, 71, 72, 73, 74, 76, 77. Position 21 is 64. Position 24 is 71. Position 25 is 72. The book notes that several percentile formulas exist. Use this one, i = (k/100)(n+1), for this section.

Fourteen ordered values

Q1, the median, and Q3

Lower half. The middle value is Q1.

1, 1, 2, 2, 4, 6, 6.8

Q1 = 2

Upper half. The middle value is Q3.

7.2, 8, 8.3, 9, 10, 10, 11.5

Q3 = 9

The median is (6.8 + 7.2) / 2 = 7. The IQR is 9 − 2 = 7, the spread of the middle half.

Unordered start: 1, 11.5, 6, 7.2, 4, 8, 9, 10, 6.8, 8.3, 2, 2, 10, 1. n = 14, so the median is the average of the 7th and 8th ordered values and is not itself in either half. In this example Q1 and Q3 are values in the data. The median, 7, is not.

Example 2.13 · 13 house prices

A value past the fence

The middle price, the 7th of 13

$488,800

IQR = 649,000 − 308,750

$340,250

1.5 × 340,250 = 510,375.

Fences: 308,750 − 510,375 = −201,625, and 649,000 + 510,375 = 1,159,375.

$5,500,000 is the only price above 1,159,375. It is a potential outlier.

Ordered prices: 114950, 158000, 230500, 387000, 389950, 479000, 488800, 529000, 575000, 639000, 659000, 1095000, 5500000. Q1 is the median of the lower six, (230500 + 387000) / 2 = 308750. Q3 is the median of the upper six, (639000 + 659000) / 2 = 649000. Use these printed quartiles. A value is a potential outlier if it is below Q1 minus 1.5 IQR or above Q3 plus 1.5 IQR. Example 2.14 · the same exam, two classes

Compare the middle halves

DAY

Min 32 · Q1 56 · median 74.5

Q3 82.5 · max 99

IQR 26.5. No outliers.

NIGHT

Min 25.5 · Q1 78 · median 81

Q3 89 · max 98

IQR 11. Outliers 25.5 and 45.

The night scores sit higher, and their middle half is tighter. The day fences are 16.25 and 122.25. The night fences are 61.5 and 105.5.

Day IQR = 82.5 - 56 = 26.5. Night IQR = 89 - 78 = 11. 1.5 times 26.5 is 39.75, so the day fences are 56 - 39.75 = 16.25 and 82.5 + 39.75 = 122.25. 1.5 times 11 is 16.5, so the night fences are 78 - 16.5 = 61.5 and 89 + 16.5 = 105.5. 45 and 25.5 are below 61.5. No night score is above 105.5.
