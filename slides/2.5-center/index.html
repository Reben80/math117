Example 2.26 · 40 patients

The median has a location

Where the median sits

(40 + 1) / 2 = 20.5

Between the 20th and 21st ages. Both are 24, so the median is 24.

The mean of the 40 ages is 23.6 years. The location 20.5 is not the median. The median is the age that sits there.

Ordered ages, 40 of them, from 3, 4, 8, 8 up to 44, 44, 47. The 20th and 21st are the two 24s. Calculator: clear L1, STAT EDIT, 1-Var Stats. x-bar = 23.6, Med = 24. If n is odd, the location (n+1)/2 is a single ordered value. If n is even, average the two middle values.

Example 2.27 · a town of 50 people

One income pulls the mean

The mean

(49 × 30,000 + 5,000,000) / 50

$129,400

The median

49 people earn this.

$30,000

The median is the better center. The income of $5,000,000 is an outlier, and the mean follows it.

The book states the median, 30000, and does not print the mean. 49 times 30000 is 1470000. Add 5000000 to get 6470000. Divide by 50 to get 129400. Try It 2.27: one house is worth 2500000, 29 are worth 280000, and the other 30 of 60 are worth 315000. Same question. The median is the better center.

The mode

The value that appears most

EXAMPLE 2.28

Twenty exam scores. 72 appears five times.

The mode is 72.

EXAMPLE 2.29

430, 430, 480, 480, 495.

Two modes. Bimodal.

The mode works for words too. In red, red, red, green, green, yellow, purple, black, blue, the mode is red. A weight-loss ad can quote a mean loss of 6 pounds while the mode is 2 pounds.

The 20 scores: 50, 53, 59, 59, 63, 63, 72, 72, 72, 72, 72, 76, 78, 81, 83, 84, 84, 84, 90, 93. 84 appears three times, so 72 is the only mode. Try It 2.28, books checked out, the mode is 7, which appears four times. Try It 2.29, credit scores 680, 680, 700, 720, 720, is also bimodal.

One more sentence

Larger samples pull the mean toward μ

The Law of Large Numbers: take larger and larger samples, and the sample mean is very likely to get closer to the population mean.

The book returns to this later. Today, the point is what the mean is.

In practice a calculator or software does the arithmetic. Read the mean and the median, then say what they tell you.

Keystrokes if someone asks: STAT 4 to clear L1, STAT EDIT to type, STAT CALC 1-Var Stats. x-bar is the sample mean. Med is the median. A sampling distribution is a relative-frequency picture of many sample means. Do not open that chapter today.
